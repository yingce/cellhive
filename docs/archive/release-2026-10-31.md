## What changed

`CellHiveLifecycle.status/retire` are platform control RPCs into the dynamic
worker, not tenant invocations. The fork records the aborting `retire` call as an
exception, so Tail exported it as `category=function_request` and Vector
classified it as `CELLHIVE_WORKER_RETIRED`, even though retirement only runs when
the child reports idle and no tenant request is interrupted.

`workerd/platform/log-tail.js` now filters `CellHiveLifecycle` records before
they enter the tail ring and on the read path. Real `CellHiveManagedFetch`
aborts still surface as `CELLHIVE_WORKER_RETIRED`.

## Why the previous release still showed the error

The 2026-10-30 release (`251725a`) fixed the in-flight retirement race. The
remaining `CELLHIVE_WORKER_RETIRED` rows were a separate classification bug:

- 48h SLS: 19 rows; 18 had `payload.event_type=""` (lifecycle RPC), the only
  `fetch` row was the `smoke-vector-mapping` version `"test"` smoke case.
- `vwork-80/keeper-authority`, 13:00–13:47Z: 768 real fetch invocations all
  `outcome=ok`; only lifecycle-RPC exceptions.
- APISIX 24h for `/functions/v1/keeper-authority/*`: 860 requests, no 5xx.
- Reproduced locally with the deployed workerd binary: version switch emits
  `CellHiveLifecycle.status` ok, `CellHiveLifecycle.retire` exception, and
  `CellHiveManagedFetch` fetch ok. After the fix, the lifecycle records are
  absent from the ring and an 8-way concurrent switch left all sampled fetches
  ok.
