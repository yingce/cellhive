Image: `sgccr.ccs.tencentyun.com/vwork/cellhive@sha256:c65a26b74296ecd78a127bc0a12fc8c03938dd7485da677f559716786d5175eb`

## Gates

Before the release: `make build`, `make vet`, `make js-test`, full `make test`,
`-race` on `internal/scopedtoken`, `internal/server` and `internal/userruntime`,
crash/owner/takeover E2E, HK COS `performance-dual`, `routing-safety`, `workflow`,
`batch-recovery`, COS contract 7/7, and k3d fleet/fault/A-B-C. The S3/MinIO gate
could not run because the pinned image could not be pulled from quay.io; this is
an environment limitation, not a pass.

## Rollout

Both regions used agent-first Helm upgrades with the formal wrapper:

- SG revision **78** (cell-agent only) then **79** (global image).
- USW revision **62** (cell-agent only) then **63** (global image).

Every business Pod reported the new imageID, Ready=true and restartCount=0 after
the final revision. No PVC or spool was deleted.

## Acceptance

For each region, through pod/service port-forward:

1. 20 direct KV PUT/GET pairs (10 TTL).
2. A temporary Worker binding `{"type":"kv","name":"KV","id":"main"}` (env key
   differs from the resource id) with 10 KV binding PUT/GET pairs. This covers
   the pinned-resource signing fixed by `49133b1`.
3. Temporary application deleted with HTTP 200.

The final 60-second log window had no WARN/ERROR/panic/fatal lines in either
region. SG emitted transient cron timer-dispatch timeouts during Pod turnover;
USW emitted the known deleted-resource waker warning. See the operations record
for details.

Evidence: operations repo commit `cb985bd`,
`/opt/cellhive-helm/reports/release-2026-10-28/`.

These are functional smoke tests, not RPS, crash-recovery, or historical-data
audits.
