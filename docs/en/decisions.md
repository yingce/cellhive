CellHive records every design decision as an ADR. **The full ADR text is written in Chinese**: [`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md) is the authoritative record. This page is a selected English index of titles and one-line summaries; read the Chinese record for the full rationale, evidence and cost of each decision.

| ADR-309 | Safe GC Operations, L1 Generations and Cleanup Atomicity | In implementation and acceptance, not deployed. | ADR-308 | Epoch Restore Lower Bound and Reader-Pin/Tombstone Deletion | In implementation and acceptance, not deployed. Readers persist leased pins, retirement seals an epoch and drains pins, Safe deletion requires a due per-key tombstone plus seal/no-live-pins, and `RetireOptions.Safe` is explicit while the production default remains DEFERRED. |
| ADR-307 | Defer Physical Replication GC by Default | Contract first; implementation and acceptance in progress, not deployed. Production background never opts in; intervals cannot bypass. Per-delete Verify stops observed loss but is not an atomic fence; optional payload ETag does not prove owner safety. Preserve marker/link/immutable epoch-cut/snapshot-started/done/unknown names in all modes. ReportDeferredGC/GCSkipped are deterministic; no pending-deletion registry; bundle/assets ops GC unchanged. Immutable cuts, complete manifest/index proof, cut-before-prune and large-marker-before-Serve fixes remain in progress. Bucket growth and historical List cost remain; online GC still needs generation descriptors, monotonic epoch references and reader pinning. |
| ADR-306 | Safe Bounds for Performance Caches and I/O | Incremental local acceptance passed; uncommitted and not deployed.
